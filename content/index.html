The one rule

You type or say what you want. The agent does the work. You check it.

Every page gives you a Say this prompt to copy or dictate to the agent. You click things yourself only where no agent can, like creating your account or signing in.

Change the site title to "A History of St. Petersburg" and show me what you changed.

What you'll end with

Your own site at https://your-username.github.io/your-repo/. The running example in this guide turns the Tampa history site into a history of your own city, such as St. Petersburg in a repo called history-of-st-pete.

These are real, live sites. The Tampa site and the Playbook are the two starters this guide uses.

The route

The five steps

Step 1 · GitHubCreate your GitHub accountFree. Your username becomes part of your site's address. Step 2 · Set up your agentApp or VS Code?The app path needs nothing installed. The VS Code path works on your computer. Step 3 · Just enough GitRepos, forks, commits and pushesThe few words you need, and the prompts that do the work. Step 4 · Make it yoursPick a starter and rewrite itTell the agent your topic. It edits, you check. Step 5 · PublishTurn on GitHub PagesYour site goes live. Then update it and share the link.

What you need

  • A GitHub account. It's free. Step 1 walks you through it.
  • A paid AI plan. Claude Pro or higher for Claude Code, or ChatGPT Plus or higher for Codex. The free Claude plan doesn't include Claude Code.
  • A phone or a computer. The app path works from a phone or a browser. The VS Code path needs a computer.
